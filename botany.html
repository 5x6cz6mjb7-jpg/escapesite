1687 Physic Garden

I started with the oldest layer. The garden began as a physic garden more than three centuries ago, meant to provide plant material for the teaching of medicine at Trinity. That detail matters. Before anyone called it beautiful, useful or academic, it was a place where plants were connected to bodies, treatment, dosage and risk. The old records are incomplete. No one even seems certain which plants were grown in the earliest garden. That absence is useful. Empty spaces in an archive are not empty. They are invitations.
My note: a garden built for medicine can also teach other lessons.
