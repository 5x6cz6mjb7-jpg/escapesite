M / Q tracking note

Recovered loose page // N. Thorne // after Jason’s death

They were not hard to find once I stopped looking for agents and started looking for habits.

M leaves structure everywhere: times, routes, repeated decisions. Q leaves traces in systems: repair logs, device registrations, curious little errors.

I followed the overlap. A name in an old municipal request. A timing pattern. A message that should have been deleted but was only moved.

They thought Jason was the ghost in the machine. That helped.

By the time they watched the first transmission, I already knew where to push. Not where they lived. Not yet. But where they would go when frightened.

Fear is a route. Love is a map. Grief is a key.

— N.
